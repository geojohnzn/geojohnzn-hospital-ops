# Hospital Ops Demo Suite

## intro
One system for a multispecialty hospital. Records, clinics, beds, documentation, quality and operations, in a single view.

## overview
[#tiles] The executive overview opens on the numbers leadership asks about every morning: census, emergency waits, clinic visits, theatre use and revenue. [#units] Bed occupancy is shown unit by unit, so pressure is visible at a glance, [#alerts] and anything that needs a decision rises to the top.

## clinics
[#clinic] At the front desk, the clinic list shows who is waiting, who is with the doctor, and who has been seen. [#beds] The bed board shows every bed in the hospital, unit by unit. [#left] When a patient goes home, one click marks the bed for cleaning, [#clean] and the counts update for everyone.

## scribe
[#transcript] In the consultation room, the doctor simply talks with the patient, in English or Arabic. [#draft] One click turns the conversation into a draft note. [#soap] Subjective, objective, assessment and plan. [#codes] Diagnosis and procedure codes come with the exact words that support each one. [#sign] The doctor reviews, edits and signs. Nothing reaches the record without them.

## quality
[#checks] For {Jawda|Jow-dah}, the quality team loads its extract as it is, messy columns and all, and sees what was cleaned. [#table] All eight infection indicators are calculated by quarter, with every numerator and denominator on show. [#chart] A monthly view separates normal ups and downs from the month that needs a case review.

## journey
[#ribbon] Patient journey shows where each patient's time goes today. Care is solid, waiting is striped. [#slider] As the changes roll out, [#full] waiting shrinks, and a typical emergency stay falls by almost three hours. [#capacity] Clinician hours follow the times patients actually arrive, with the same total hours.

## forecast
[#fc] The forecast looks two weeks ahead at emergency arrivals, [#bedsf] and a year ahead at bed demand, with and without the redesign.

## outro {hold=2}
Built on synthetic data. Ready for your pilot.
